# The three papers

One study, three separable results. They are reported separately because they answer different
questions, rest on different experiments, and are useful to different readers.

| | paper | asks | uses the optimizer? | pages |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | [`p1-conditional-lever/`](p1-conditional-lever/) — *Whether Optimizing the Loop Beats Optimizing the Prompt Depends on One Number* | Does evolving a self-refine loop's verifier beat evolving its generation prompt? | yes, it is the subject | 39 |
| **II** | [`p2-repair-not-detection/`](p2-repair-not-detection/) — *What a Checker Cannot Buy Is the Repair* | Where is the loop's own ceiling: detection or repair? | **no** — every measurement is arm A, which declares no evolvable genes | 23 |
| **III** | [`p3-silent-no-op/`](p3-silent-no-op/) — *Your Loop Optimizer Is Not Optimizing Your Loop* | How can a componentized treatment silently not apply, and what catches it? | yes, as the thing that failed | 20 |

## Which paper answers your question

**"Should I point my prompt optimizer at my critique prompt?"** → **I**. The answer is conditional
on p₀, the base rate of first drafts that already satisfy every requirement: at p₀ = 0.267 it gains
0.060 held-out, at p₀ = 0.067 it does not measurably help, and the 0.102 swing between them is what
carries the result rather than either endpoint.

**"Should I buy a better verifier for my self-refine loop?"** → **II**. Probably not. A *perfect*
inference-time checker is statistically indistinguishable from the loop's near-random self-critique
(−0.0027, *t* = 0.33) and scores 0.042 *below* a deterministic text-statistics tool. What pays is
the repair instruction: naming which constraint failed is worth +0.003, a concrete measured edit is
worth +0.0613.

**"How do I know my componentized optimization experiment actually ran?"** → **III**. Print one
integer per component: whether the optimizer's output differs from the seed. Our first fifteen
contrast runs did not, and the critique gene was byte-identical to the seed in 15/15 winning
genomes.

## Why II is a separate paper, not a section of I

Paper II's six experiments invoke the optimizer **zero times**. Every one is
`run_validation_study.py --arms A`, which scores a fixed genome and runs no search. Remove GEPA from
this project entirely and Paper II loses not one number. It is a harness ablation of a self-refine
loop, not a result about optimizers, and it has the study's best-supported effects.

### How the checks survived the split

All of them read through `scripts/paper_sources.py` rather than a hard-coded path — a guard that
silently narrowed its own scope is precisely the defect Paper III is about.

- **Concatenated view** (`all_tex()`): the ~155 pinned-phrase checks and the four coverage guards.
  A sentence that survived the split into *some* paper is still found; one the split dropped yields
  `NaN` and fails loudly.
- **Per-paper view** (`paper_tex(slug)`): the spine checks (each paper still carries its own
  headline numbers), the cross-paper agreement checks (a number quoted by two papers must be the
  same number in both), and each paper's corrections count.
- **Split proof** (`verify_split_coverage.py`): all 318 quantities in the frozen pre-split draft
  are carried by at least one paper, or superseded by a replacement that is *verified present*, or
  explicitly dropped with a reason. Zero are dropped.

### One of those four guards was inert until 2026-08-05

`test_paper_prose_coverage` asks whether every decimal the papers state in prose is one a check
derives from an artifact. It answered yes vacuously for its whole life: it rebuilt the set of
derived values by replaying the checker's regex literals over the document, and one of those
literals is the bare capture fragment `([\d.]+)` that the table-row patterns are composed from.
Compiled alone it matched 3937 numbers, so the bound set *was* the document and the guard asserted
that the papers agree with themselves.

It concealed seven orphans. All seven turned out to be correct once derived, so **none of the seven
changed a published number** — but they were unpinned, and two were the exact defect the guard's own
docstring was written about, including one that sat as a literal inside the pattern checking its own
σ quotient. The repair replaced the replay with the check list itself, which already records every
pinned value, and added `test_the_guard_reports_an_injected_orphan`: it feeds in a decimal known to
be underivable and demands a failure. The size floors in `test_coverage_is_not_vacuous` could never
have caught this, because an over-broad bound set is *larger* than a correct one and clears every
floor.

Pinning them did surface one false *sentence*, now Paper I's thirteenth register entry: the
appendix called all eight C1 tasks "the only ones on which the two genes' verdicts differ" while
conceding two clauses earlier that both genes demanded a fix on five of them. The verdicts differ on
three. No quantity moved — the −0.009/−0.023 split stands — and all three stratum sizes are now
derived from the replay.

## Shared across all three

- **One artifact release.** 43 study runs, 225 replicate scorings, 267 cassettes. Four workers,
  and the split is the thing to know before reading any of them: **33** runs are on
  `openai/gpt-4.1-mini`, the worker every released curve is measured on; **1** is the
  `openai/gpt-4.1-nano` worker swap (Paper I's run 11); **8** are on
  `groq/llama-3.3-70b-versatile` and **1** on `groq/llama-3.1-8b-instant`. Those last **9** are a
  second-worker replication line bought on a Groq credential (`validation-study-groq-*`,
  `validation-study-splice-*`). A different worker is a different baseline, so **none of that
  line's numbers extends a released curve** — it corroborates rather than continues. All three
  papers draw on it, and each says which part it is entitled to: Paper II
  builds its cross-worker section on the six detector cells, Paper I cites the collapse of the
  `p₀` ladder on that worker as evidence for an open design problem and explicitly declines to
  carry its own lever there, and Paper III takes defects 10–11 from the instruments that broke
  while it was being bought.
- **One corrections register, split three ways.** Each paper carries the withdrawals bearing on its
  own claims and states its own count; the full register across all three is the union. Every count
  is derived from the entries actually listed, because it drifted twice during the study.
- **One reproducibility guarantee, and it now covers the whole release.** Every one of the 43 runs
  holds a recorded status, and the three states partition it: **25** replay bit-for-bit from
  cassette at `$0`, **13** are registered with frozen CLI args and labelled superseded — each with
  its own stated reason, never a blanket one — and **5** BigCodeBench runs are listed as
  unreplayable because executing model code re-rolls unseeded randomness and shifts the
  content-addressed prompt key. `tests/test_verify_reproducibility.py` asserts that partition is
  total, so a run cannot supply a published number while holding no status at all. It reached 25
  from 14 by registration alone: eleven runs were already replaying and nothing said so.
